# Privacy policy for bid Pointer

Last updated: 2026-11-09

bid Pointer turns what you point at on a website, and the client emails you choose, into tasks in your own ClickUp workspace. It has no server of its own. It sends data only when you press send, and only to ClickUp and, if you add a Z.ai key, to Z.ai. We, the makers of Pointer, never receive any of it. Pointer has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

## What Pointer sends, and to whom

Nothing leaves your browser until you have agreed to this in Pointer's settings (in Firefox, when you install it), and then only when you send something.

| When | What | To |
|---|---|---|
| You send a note about a page | What you wrote; a screenshot of the visible page with the spot marked, or a screen recording you made instead; the page's address and title; the text of the element you clicked and where it sits on the page; your browser and window size | ClickUp, as a task or a comment in the list you choose |
| You press Send to ClickUp in Gmail or HubSpot | The emails you have open: their text, senders, dates, images and attached files; for a HubSpot ticket, its name, owner, priority and contact | ClickUp, as tasks |
| You send an SEO check | The page's address and the findings you ticked, with the texts and tags they're about | ClickUp, as a task |
| Only if you added a Z.ai key: you send a note or an email | A note's text with the page's address and title and the clicked element's text, to rewrite it; an email's text, to split it into tasks. Never screenshots, recordings or files. | Z.ai, whose GLM model writes the reply |

Pointer sends your ClickUp token with every request to ClickUp, and your Z.ai key with every request to Z.ai, so they know it's you. It also reads from ClickUp, as you, the spaces, lists, people and open Pointer tasks it needs for its pickers and to add comments.

ClickUp and Z.ai handle what they receive under your own agreements with them: [ClickUp's privacy policy](https://clickup.com/terms/privacy), [Z.ai](https://z.ai).

## What Pointer reads in your browser

- On every page, a small script checks the page's address against your list of sites and, if you named your agency's domains, looks for them in the page's footer. That's all it does on other sites, and nothing leaves the browser.
- On your sites, and wherever you start Pointer, it reads the page you're on to name what you click and, for the SEO check, the page's tags and texts. The SEO check also loads the site's robots.txt and sitemap and, when you ask it to check links, the pages it links to, straight from your browser.
- In Gmail and HubSpot, it reads an email or ticket only when you press Send to ClickUp.

## What Pointer keeps on your device

In your browser's extension storage, on this device only: your ClickUp token, your Z.ai key and your settings; the projects you last used per site and per sender; your last 20 tasks; notes and their screenshots waiting to be published as a page task; the IDs (not the contents) of emails already sent to ClickUp; and a list of open Pointer tasks for Comment Mode. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

## Setup codes

A setup code is a team's settings written as one line of text, made and read inside Pointer's settings page. Pointer never sends it anywhere: you copy it and share it yourself. A code never holds a ClickUp token, so everyone connects their own. It holds the Z.ai key only if you choose to include it, and then it's as private as the key itself.

## Your choices

- Withdraw your agreement in Pointer's settings at any time. Pointer then sends nothing until you agree again.
- Remove your Z.ai key to stop sending anything to Z.ai.
- Regenerate your ClickUp token in ClickUp to cut Pointer off at once.
- Tasks, comments and files Pointer created live in your ClickUp workspace; change or delete them there.

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

Pointer's use of the information it receives adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to create the tasks and comments you ask for, is never transferred for any other purpose, and is never used for advertising or credit decisions.
