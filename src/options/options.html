Connect ClickUp

Pointer creates tasks as you, so it needs your personal ClickUp API token. It takes about a minute, and the token stays in this browser.

  1. 1

    Open your ClickUp settings

    This opens ClickUp's Apps settings in a new tab. Log in first if ClickUp asks you to.

    Open ClickUp settings

    Prefer to click there yourself? In ClickUp, click your avatar, then Settings, then Apps.

  2. 2

    Copy your API token

    Find the API Token section. If it's empty, click Generate. Then click Copy.

    Already have a token? Click Copy, not Regenerate. Regenerating replaces the old token, and anything else that uses it stops working.

  3. 3

What can Pointer do with this token?

It creates tasks, attaches screenshots and reads your projects and team, all as you. The token is stored only in this browser and sent only to ClickUp. To cut Pointer off at any time, click Regenerate in ClickUp; the old token stops working at once.

Where Pointer shows up

Pointer starts by itself on localhost and on your sites, which count as staging. Anywhere else, click Pointer's toolbar button or press the shortcut.

One per line. * stands for any part of the address, so *-staging.vercel.app covers every project's staging site. Takes effect on the next page you open.

If your clients' live sites credit you in their footer (a link to your site, "Powered by …"), Pointer starts on those too, as live sites. Emails from these domains count as your team's in Send to ClickUp. Leave empty to skip.

What Pointer sends, and where

Pointer has no server of its own. It sends things only when you press send, and only to your ClickUp workspace and, if you add a key, to Z.ai.

Hide the details
  • To ClickUp, when you send a note: what you wrote, a screenshot of the page with the spot marked (or your screen recording instead), the page's address and title, the text of what you clicked and where it sits on the page, and your browser and window size.
  • To ClickUp, when you press Send to ClickUp in Gmail or HubSpot: the emails you have open, with their senders, dates, images and attached files, and for a HubSpot ticket its name, owner and contact.
  • To ClickUp, when you send an SEO check: the page's address and the findings you ticked, with the texts and tags they're about.
  • To Z.ai, only if you add a Z.ai key: a note's text with the page's address and title and what you clicked, to rewrite it; and the emails' text, to split them into tasks. Never screenshots, recordings or files.
  • Kept in this browser: your ClickUp token, your Z.ai key and your settings. They're sent only to ClickUp and Z.ai, to sign in. Nothing goes to bid or anyone else, and Pointer collects no analytics.
